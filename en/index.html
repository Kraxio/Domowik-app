FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers without hiding important limitations.

Another question? Contact us →
Do I need to create an account?+

No. Domowik does not require an account. App data is stored locally on the device.

Does Domowik work without internet?+

Core features work without internet. A connection may be needed, for example, when buying Premium on Google Play or sending a deadline to Google Calendar.

How do I move data to a new phone?+

The easiest way is over WiFi: with both phones on the same network, pick “Move to another phone”, enter a short code and the data goes across directly — no internet, account or cloud. You can also create a full JSON backup in Settings and import it on the new device. Data does not sync automatically in the background.

What can I store with an item?+

Name, location, category, note, quantity, value, purchase date and place, photos and linked deadlines.

What is different with Premium?+

Premium removes the item limit and expands photos, finances, saved lists, templates and the habits module.

Which languages does the app support?+

The interface is available in Polish, English, German, French, Spanish, Ukrainian and Czech.

Does Domowik collect any data about me?+

No. The app has no server to send anything to, and contains no ads or analytics tools. Everything you enter — items, amounts, photos, deadlines — is saved in the phone’s memory and never leaves it unless you export a backup yourself. The details are in the privacy policy.

Can I lock access to the app?+

Yes. Entry can be locked with a PIN code or a fingerprint. The backup file can also be encrypted with a password before you pass it on.

Can I keep a car service history?+

Yes. Add the car as an item and append entries to it: date, description and cost. That builds a timeline — from the purchase through oil and brake pad changes to the last inspection. The same approach works for a bike, a washing machine or a boiler.

Does Domowik remind me about deadlines?+

Yes. Each deadline can carry a reminder set in advance — on the day itself or 1, 3, 7 or 14 days earlier. Notifications work locally, and tapping one opens that deadline.
