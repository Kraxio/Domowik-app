Privacy

No server, no account, no analytics

Domowik has no server to send your data to. There is no account to create, no ads and no analytics. Your inventory, amounts and photos stay in the phone's memory — including when you switch the internet off.

We collect no data on our servers — there is no central database that could leak and no account anyone could take over. That is not a promise, but a consequence of how the app is built.

Important: data does not sync automatically. Create a backup or move your data over WiFi before changing phones or uninstalling the app.
